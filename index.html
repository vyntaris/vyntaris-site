Settings

All in one place

Press Ctrl+F and type what you're looking for.

  1. Quickshell

    The island

    Everything opens from here: settings, search, sound, music, the power menu.

  2. Quickshell

    The bar

    Workspaces, Wi-Fi, battery, and a dot when an app is using your mic or camera.

  3. Hyprland

    The windows

    They arrange themselves. You don't have to drag anything around.

  4. Colour

    The wallpaper

    Pick a colour and the wallpaper, your apps, Firefox and the login screen change with it.

  5. Keys

    One key to start

    Tap SUPER and start typing. That's most of what you need to know.

  6. NetworkManager · BlueZ

    Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, your phone

    Connect right from the panel. Your phone pairs too, through KDE Connect.

  7. Arch Linux

    Underneath

    It's plain Arch: same kernel, same pacman. Drivers install on their own.

  8. Power

    The battery

    Power saver turns on at 20%. Charging can stop at 80% if your laptop supports it.

  9. Btrfs

    The disk

    It gets trimmed every week and checked for damage every month, without you asking.

  10. Game mode

    Games

    Game mode starts when a game does: quiet notifications, FPS in the island.

  11. PipeWire

    Sound

    All your outputs and inputs in one panel, plus noise suppression for the mic.

  12. Timeshift

    A copy before every update

    The system saves itself first. Your files aren't part of it.

  13. update-guard

    Bad updates undo themselves

    If the system won't start three times in a row, you get the old one back.
